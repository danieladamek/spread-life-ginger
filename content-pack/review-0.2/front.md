# Abstract

Ginger (*Zingiber officinale*) is read here through three records at once: the documents that describe its use, the human trials that test it, and the biology of the plant. The plant is a sterile, clonally propagated cultigen, unknown in the wild, whose genome was assembled in 2021 and whose key gingerol-making enzyme was demonstrated only in 2026 [@cheng2021; @pena2026]. Its pungent gingerols convert to shogaols on drying and heating, so fresh and dried ginger are different materials, a distinction Chinese medicine drew centuries before the chemistry [@connell1969; @stuart1911]. In people, the best-supported effect is on nausea in pregnancy; evidence for other uses is positive in direction and low in certainty, the motion-sickness trials conflict, and harm has not been detected in trials or a large cohort though regulators remain cautious [@crichton2022; @heitmann2012; @emamonograph2025]. After oral ginger the free compounds are not found in blood, which complicates every proposed mechanism [@zick2008]. The documentary record runs from Dioscorides and the *Suśruta Saṃhitā* through the medieval spice trade, the Caribbean plantations and the ginger-beer bottle to the "jake leg" poisoning of 1930 [@yule1903; @bhishagratna1907; @flueckiger1879; @aram2015; @morgan1976]. This review is a snapshot as of 24 November 2026; most scientific sources were read as abstracts, and historical sources through scanned public-domain editions.

## 1. Why ginger, and why three records at once

Ginger is unusual in being at once an ancient trade good, a kitchen staple, a regulated herbal medicine and a sequenced crop. Each of those identities has its own literature, and they seldom cite one another. A historian can say what a fourteenth-century cook did with powdered ginger, a trialist whether a capsule eased nausea, and a plant biologist which genes switch on in a swelling rhizome. None of the three can say alone whether the cook's dish contained what the trialist tested, or why the plant makes it in the first place. This review sets the records side by side and marks, section by section, where one supports, complicates or contradicts another. It reports what its sources say and where they stop, and it labels its own inferences as such. <!-- framing -->
