## What it is
A meta-analysis combines trials; an umbrella review combines meta-analyses. The same trials can sit under several reviews, so many reviews do not mean many independent experiments: seven meta-analyses of pregnancy nausea shared 49% of their studies [33].

GRADE rates how far a pooled result can be trusted. For ginger the ratings run from high, for nausea in pregnancy, to very low, for several metabolic outcomes [5].

A wide confidence interval means the data cannot distinguish no effect from a large one, as with miscarriage risk in the pregnancy trials [6].

## How this guide uses it
Sections 8 to 10 give each pooled result with its certainty rating where one exists, and say when an interval is too wide to support a conclusion. <!-- framing -->
