## What it is
Feeding labelled compounds to ginger showed that phenylalanine is made into ferulic acid, which condenses with malonate and hexanoate to give 6-dehydrogingerdione; two reductions then give 6-gingerol [130]. The length of the fatty acid that joins sets whether the product is 6-, 8- or 10-gingerol [130]. In 2026 the condensation was matched to a ginger enzyme, the polyketide synthase ZoPKS2 [2]. The reductions that finish the job have not yet been tied to enzymes in the sources read [2].

## How this guide uses it
Section 12 follows this route through labelling, enzyme assays, gene surveys and genomes. Figure 6 marks each step as shown, proposed or chemical. Shogaol is not on the plant's pathway in any study read here; it forms when ginger is dried or cooked. <!-- framing -->
